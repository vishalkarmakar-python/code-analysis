# AI Engineer Learning Roadmap for SAP Professionals

## Overview

This roadmap is designed for SAP ABAP Consultants with SAPUI5 experience and Python skills who want to transition into AI engineering. The schedule is structured to accommodate a busy professional with:

- **Weekdays (Monday-Friday)**: 2 hours of learning per day
- **Weekends (Saturday-Sunday)**: 6 hours of learning per day

This equals 22 hours per week, or approximately 88 hours per month of dedicated learning time.

## Tips for Success

1. **Consistency over intensity**: It's better to do your 2 hours every day than to skip days and try to catch up.
2. **Apply learnings to real problems**: Try to relate each concept to your SAP experience.
3. **Build incrementally**: Each week should build on previous knowledge.
4. **Join communities**: Interact with others on the same path to stay motivated.
5. **Document your journey**: Keep notes, code repositories, and blog about your progress.
6. **Connect with professionals**: Network with both SAP and AI professionals to gain insights.
7. **Focus on your strengths**: Leverage your SAP domain knowledge as your unique advantage.

## Adjusting the Plan

This roadmap is a guideline, not a rigid structure. Feel free to:

- Spend more time on topics that are challenging
- Move faster through familiar concepts
- Pivot based on your growing interests within AI
- Adjust based on emerging technologies and trends

The goal is to build a solid foundation while creating practical projects that showcase your unique combination of SAP and AI expertise.
